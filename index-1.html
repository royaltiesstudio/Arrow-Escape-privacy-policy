Simple summaryArrow Escape is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection. Gameplay progress and settings may be stored locally, while Google Play Games Services may process information required for supported gaming features.

1. Overview

Arrow Escape: Puzzle Game is developed and published by Royalties Studio.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the information processed by the game limited to what is reasonably necessary for its functionality, security, support, and supported gaming features.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be handled when you use Arrow Escape.

2. Information We Handle

The game does not require you to provide personal information such as your phone number, UPI ID, or home address to play the normal game.

Gameplay InformationLevel progress, completed levels, gameplay-related progress, achievements, scores, and other game-state information.
Game SettingsPreferences and settings needed to provide a consistent gameplay experience.
Play Games InformationInformation associated with Google Play Games when you use supported Play Games features.
Technical InformationTechnical information may be processed by platform services when required for authentication, security, compatibility, or diagnostics.

3. Local Game Data

Arrow Escape may store gameplay information locally on your device.

Depending on the features enabled in the game, this may include:

  • Level progress
  • Game settings
  • Gameplay inventory and counts
  • Other information necessary to continue your game session

Local data is used to provide the game experience and is not intended to be used as a separate personal profile.

4. Google Play Games Services

Arrow Escape may use Google Play Games Services for supported gaming functionality.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Player authentication
  • Achievements
  • Leaderboards
  • Cloud game progress / Saved Games

Google may process information associated with these services according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the game or its supported platform services may be used to:

  • Provide game functionality.
  • Save and restore gameplay progress.
  • Provide achievements and leaderboard features.
  • Synchronize supported game progress.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve game stability and functionality.
  • Respond to support requests.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information handled by the game and its services.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Advertising

Current status: Advertising is not currently enabled in the current version of Arrow Escape.

Royalties Studio may introduce advertising in a future version of the game.

If advertising is introduced, the game may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob or another advertising provider.

Depending on the advertising technology used, advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, app information, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated to accurately describe the advertising services, information processed, purposes, and applicable user choices.

We will not describe advertising as active when advertising is not actually enabled in the game.

8. Third-Party Services

Some features may depend on services provided by third parties.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Current or future third-party services may include:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play platform services
  • Advertising providers, if advertising is introduced in the future
